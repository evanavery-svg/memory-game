When you play

    How to play

    1. A pattern of tiles flashes for a moment.
    2. When it fades, tap every tile you saw.
    3. Clear the board to advance — each one is a little harder.
    4. Clear a board with no misses to build a combo multiplier.
    5. Combos earn Peek and Skip power-ups — use them when a board is tough.
    6. A wrong tap costs a life. Lose them all and the run ends.

    Endless — go as far as you can, solo or two-player (pass and play; highest score wins).
    Daily — everyone gets the same boards each day; one run, shareable result.
    Order — tiles flash one at a time; tap them back in order.
